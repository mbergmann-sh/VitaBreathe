VitaBreathe – licence and disclaimer
====================================

IMPORTANT: VitaBreathe is a personal diary for breathing exercises and NOT a
medical device. It does not make diagnoses and does not replace medical advice
or respiratory physiotherapy. Use at your own risk. Stop an exercise
immediately if you feel dizzy, unwell or short of breath, and follow your
medical emergency plan.

Your data is stored encrypted with your master password. Nobody can recover a
forgotten master password – make regular backups. Uninstalling keeps your
database and your settings.
